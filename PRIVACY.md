# Calmer Card privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Calmer Card is a browser extension that changes how the Swapcard attendee web app (app.swapcard.com) looks and works in your own browser. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with Swapcard.

## What we collect

Nothing. Calmer Card has no server, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your Swapcard data, your messages or anything about how you use the extension.

## What the extension does with your data

- **Talks only to Swapcard.** To show your calendar, inbox and profiles, Calmer Card asks Swapcard's own servers for the same information the Swapcard app already shows you, using the session you are already signed in with. Actions you take (such as accepting a request or booking a meeting) are sent to Swapcard, just as if you had used Swapcard's own buttons.
- **Stores a few preferences in your browser.** Sessions you hide, and whether to show sessions you're not attending or hidden sessions, are saved in your browser's local storage for app.swapcard.com. They stay on your device. Clearing site data for app.swapcard.com removes them.
- **Feedback is up to you.** The feedback button opens a pre-filled GitHub issue page in a new tab with your comment, the page you were on and the extension version. Nothing is sent unless you submit it on GitHub yourself, under your own account, where it will be public. If you add a screenshot, it is taken and edited on your device and copied to your clipboard so you can paste it in. Please blur other people's names, photos and messages before sharing.

## Sharing and selling

We do not sell, share or transfer any user data, because we do not collect any.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
